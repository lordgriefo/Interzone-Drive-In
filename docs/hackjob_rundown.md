## 📽️ WHAT HACKJOB ACTUALLY DOES

### 1. 📚 Reference Library
- Hundreds of tap-to-add ingredients across ~19 collapsible drawers:
  - **Camera & Lens** (anamorphic, macro, split diopter, vintage glass)
  - **Light & Color** (chiaroscuro, golden hour, neon noir, bleach bypass)
  - **Cinematic Moves** (whip pan, push-in, Dutch angle, vertigo dolly zoom)
  - **Story Engines & Tropes**
  - **Genres & Microgenres** (folk horror, giallo, cosmic dread, 90s indie)
  - **Strange / Unusual Films & Cult Oddities**
  - **Classics & World Cinema**
  - **Filmmakers & Auteurs** (Lynch, Kurosawa, Cronenberg, Kubrick, Tarkovsky)
  - **Novelists & Poets** (Burroughs, Ballard, Philip K. Dick, Shirley Jackson)
  - **Musicians & Composers** (Morricone, Portishead, Bernard Herrmann, Goblin)
  - **Screenwriters, Art Movements & Photographers**
  - **Tones, Settings, Eras, Radio/Audio Drama, TV Serials & Music Videos**
- Live search + collapsible sections for instant discovery.

### 2. 🧪 Your Mix
- Click anything to build an active influence stack.
- Add custom user tags plus a free-text catalyst/spark box.
- **"Mix It Up"**: Rolls a random 4–5 ingredient combo for instant inspiration.

### 3. 📝 Concept + Scene Beats
- AI synthesizes a structured concept: `TITLE` / `GENRE` / `LOGLINE` / `PREMISE` / `TONE` / `VISUAL PALETTE`.
- Generates numbered scene beats (3–24 scenes, from a ~3-minute short up to a 60+ minute feature start).
- Both concept and beats are fully editable and re-rollable at any step.

### 4. 📜 Screenplay Drafting
- Writes proper industry screenplay format in batches (300–375 words/scene).
- Auto-thickens thin batches, splices cleanly, resumes from the cut, and ends on `THE END`.
- Pause, edit, or continue at any point.

### 5. ✍️ Authoring Desk
- Quick-insert tags: angles, moves, lens/light/design, SFX/MUSIC.
- One-click establishing shots, camera moves, and light shifts.
- **"Unstick" Co-Writer**: Suggests 3 insertable narrative continuations when hitting writer's block.
- Live word, page, and estimated screen-time meter.

### 6. 🗣️ Table Read (Character Voices)
- Casts every speaking part dynamically:
  - **Instant Browser Voices**: Per-character assigned voice and pitch.
  - **Polished On-Device AI Voice**: Single narrator reading action and dialogue.
- Play from the top or current cursor position, pause/skip, and fine-tune playback rate.

### 7. 🔮 Style Forge
- Feed it any seed (a year, a city, a vintage camera, a painter, an event, or a made-up phrase).
- Generates a full `CONTEXT` / `GEAR` / `TOUCHSTONES` / `SPARK` dossier.
- Drop it straight into your mix or save it to your private local library (stored in browser local storage, with JSON backup/restore).

### 8. 🎨 Poster + Teaser Lab
- Picks poster styles across 14 distinct artistic lanes:
  - *Saul Bass, Bob Peak / Drew Struzan, Mondo, Noir Photo, Blockbuster, Brutalist Type, Indie, Occult Woodcut, Western Vista, Studio Ghibli, Neon VHS, Arthouse, Found-Photo...*
- Writes 3 copy-paste poster prompts and generates 3 poster images right on the page.
- Generates a 5/8/12-shot teaser shot list with `FRAME`, `MOTION`, `LIGHT`, and `AUDIO` prompts optimized for video diffusion tools (Runway, Kling, Pika, Luma Dream Machine).

### 9. 💾 Production Vault & Exports
- **Standard Exports**: Fountain (`.fountain`), proper screenplay layout PDF, plain TXT, JSON, and full ZIP bundle (auto-named from your script title).
- **Production Vault**: Auto-snapshots every draft with 60 local save slots (load, export, delete, and full vault import/export).

### 10. 🎨 6 Color Themes
- **Hackjob Default**: High-contrast black, cyan/blue, and electric orange.
- **Violet Noir**: Moody purple and deep shadows.
- **Old Hollywood Gold**: Vintage warm tungsten and brass.
- **Neon Drive-In**: Electric magenta and radioactive green.
- **Blood & Popcorn**: Grindhouse crimson and buttery yellow.
- **Matinee**: Clean, readable high-key daytime theme.
