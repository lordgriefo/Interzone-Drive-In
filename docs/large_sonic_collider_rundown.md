## The 5 Main Tabs

### 1. Sonic Sector — Music Prompt Builder
- **109 sectors, ~1000+ clickable particles.**
- **Particle Categories:**
  - Drums (Cinematic/Epic, EDM/Festival, Funk/Disco, Trap/Hip-Hop, Rock, Reggae/Dancehall/Afrobeat, Production Styles, Solo Styles)
  - Guitars, Bass, Synths (Classic Analog, Experimental/Modular, Sci-Fi/Horror)
  - Vocals (Solo, Choir, Chanting, Crooning/Vintage, FX & Processing, Traditional/Throat/Yodel/Ululation)
  - Piano & Keyboards, Wind & Brass, Strings (Bowed/Bass, Plucked/Strummed), Orchestration
  - FX (Ambient, Impact, Industrial, Radio/Sci-Fi, Insanity/Chaos, Transitions, Vocal Textures)
  - Moods, Genres, Micro-Genres, Blends & Fusion, Decade Influence, Cinematic Spaces, Spatial Audio / Advanced Spatial Design
  - Production Chaos, Harmonic Anomalies, Chorus Intensifiers, Bridge Destabilizers, Finale Strategies
  - Suno Genre Templates / Vocal Styles / Instrument Stacks / FX & Textures / Micro-Packs, LSC Anomalies / Strangelets.
- **Workflow Tools:** Search across all particles, dropdown sector jumper, drag-and-drop into Collision Chamber, Presets, My Tags, custom tag import, and JSON database export.

### 2. Lyric Lab — Lyric Prompt Builder & Vocal Isolation
- **Generator Mode:** 69 sectors across Bizarre (oddities, philosophy, mysteries, rituals), Lit (Beats, Sci-Fi, Southern Gothic, Transgressive), Emotional Perspectives (40 emotions), and 62 subgenres.
- **Analysis Engine & Studio Jam:** Deconstructs BPM, key, chords, and song DNA. Features Cyberpunk Mutation, Beck Slacker-Funk Rewrite, and co-writer personas (Strangelet Sonar, Beck, Bowie, Tom Waits, Prince, Brian Eno, Patti Smith).
- **Karaoke Booth:** Drag-and-drop MP3/WAV/FLAC/OGG files for local stem isolation of backing track vs vocal guide using phase-coherent crossover (retains kick and bass integrity without warble). Includes vocal rehearsal fader and demo groove.

### 3. Mastering Matrix Pro — 14-Stage Mastering + Matchering
- **AI Auto-Master:** One-click automated mastering with A/B compare and 60fps FFT spectrum visualizer.
- **Mastering Presets:** AI Smart Ear, Audiophile, Spatial 3D / Dolby Atmos, -14 LUFS Streaming, Club/Bass 808, Vintage Tape, Pop Polish, Lo-Fi Vinyl, Fostex X-26 Tape, Yamaha SU10 Grit, CERN 13.6 TeV Strangelet Exciter.
- **14 Tunable Processing Stages:** Headroom Trim, DC Cut, Sub Weight, Bass Body, Mud Cut, Mid Core, Attack Bite, Brilliance, Air Sheen, Tape Warmth, 3D Hologram Width, Glue Compressor, De-Harsh, Peak Limiter, True Ceiling, Master Trim.
- **Bulk EP/Album Mode:** Batch process up to 5 tracks simultaneously with per-song AI mastering and bulk WAV export.
- **Matchering (Reference Transfer):** Match acoustic balance to classic references (e.g. Beck *Sea Change* / Bob Ludwig master) or custom tracks via spectral/delta EQ transfer, loudness/crest/width deltas, and 50%/85%/100% match strength.

### 4. Pitch Portal — Audio-to-MIDI
- **Stem & File Support:** Single track or multitrack stems.
- **Detection Modes:** Polyphonic Chords & Melody, Vocal/Lead, Bassline C1-C4, Chords & Harmony, Percussive Transients.
- **Controls & Piano Roll:** Onset sensitivity, minimum note duration, tempo override, auto-key/tempo detection, interactive piano roll with scrub, and Standard MIDI (`.MID`) export for any DAW.

### 5. Sonic Decompiler — Audio-to-Prompt
- Reverse-engineers uploaded audio (MP3/WAV/FLAC/OGG) using Perchance AI and Web DSP to extract vocals, instruments, lyrics, BPM, and prompt tags into a reusable generation prompt.
